The risk

Four failure patterns that no milestone report shows.

Target missed

Adoption collapses after go-live

The system is live and the licences are running — but many users carry on exactly as before. Every percentage point of missing adoption is burnt migration budget.

4–5 people

Support load rests on a handful of people

After cutover, four or five key users carry the load for hundreds. They drop out or resign — and the process knowledge leaves with them.

Excel instead of SAP

Business units work around the new system

Wherever the new system creates friction, workarounds appear: shadow spreadsheets, parallel processes, manual handovers. The rollout is formally complete — reality runs alongside it.

Day 1 ≠ day 90

The migration runs. The master data rots.

The data transfer works technically — but without disciplined upkeep, master data degrades within weeks: wrong material masters, duplicates, empty fields.

What insivo measures

Readiness is not a status light. It is six dimensions with evidence behind each.

Key users

Are they still holding up, or about to break?

Confidential conversations with every key user surface overload and attrition risk before a key person drops out.

Adoption

Are units really working in the system?

Front-line conversations reveal workarounds, shadow systems and what usage actually looks like.

Sites

Is the rollout running the same everywhere?

Per-site and per-department readings show which unit carries the rollout and which has long fallen behind.

Productivity

Is the transition costing throughput right now?

Where cycle times break down and teams get stuck in workarounds — made visible while it is still fixable.

External partners

Are consultants delivering knowledge or hours?

Where knowledge transfer fails and dependency on external partners quietly builds.

Project status

Is the rollout really green, or only in the report?

The official status reconciled against what the teams actually experience.
