The pattern

The mandate ends. The evidence leaves with it.

20–30 interviews

The sample decides the answer

Who gets interviewed shapes the result. Twenty conversations in an organization of eight hundred people means the selection is already a hypothesis.

Six weeks

The answer arrives once the question is old

A quarter of project reality sits between fieldwork and presentation. What is in the deck was true when it was gathered.

One-off

Repeating it costs the same again

A second measurement means a second mandate. So it rarely happens — and nobody finds out whether the recommendation worked.

Nothing remains

After the project, a PDF

What you learned about the organization lives in heads and slides. The next mandate with the same client starts the fieldwork from zero.

None of these four is a consulting-quality problem. All four are a missing-technology problem.
