Control

You decide how much insivo is allowed to do.

Orchestration is a dial, not a switch. Every enterprise sets its own level — and can move it per initiative.

LEVEL 1

Recommend

insivo proposes the next action. Everything else is human.

LEVEL 2

Assist

insivo prepares the action. A human approves before anything moves.

LEVEL 3

Execute

A human authorises insivo to coordinate the intervention end to end.

LEVEL 4

Autopilot

Within policies you define, insivo coordinates low-risk interventions on its own.

Most enterprises start at level 1 or 2 and move up per initiative, not per contract.
