The problem

The migration is an IT project. The failure is not.

Target missed

Adoption collapses after go-live

The system is live and the licences are running — but many users carry on exactly as before. Every percentage point of missing adoption is burnt migration budget.

4–5 people

Support load rests on a handful of people

After cutover, four or five key users carry the load for hundreds of users. They drop out or resign — and the process knowledge leaves with them.

Excel instead of SAP

Business units work around the new system

Wherever the new system creates friction, workarounds appear: shadow spreadsheets, parallel processes, manual handovers. The rollout is formally complete — reality runs alongside it.

Day 1 ≠ day 90

The migration runs. The master data rots.

The data transfer works technically — but without disciplined upkeep, master data degrades within weeks: wrong material masters, duplicates, empty fields.

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before you start.

How does the anonymisation work?

Participants access the system via UUID tokens instead of real names. Personal details are removed automatically before analysis (SHA256 hashing), and results only appear above a minimum group size (k-anonymity ≥ 5). Conclusions about individuals are therefore impossible — including for you as the client.

Do we need works council approval?

Usually yes. §87 (1) no. 6 BetrVG makes applications subject to co-determination if they can record behaviour or performance; §94 covers questionnaires. insivo ships the required documentation — anonymisation concept, minimum group size, purpose limitation — as standard, so approval takes days rather than weeks.

How is this different from an employee survey?

A survey measures the what on a scale. insivo delivers the why: adaptive conversations with follow-up questions, turned into a root-cause analysis with prioritised actions. You do not get a satisfaction score of 4.2, you get a diagnosis you can work on.

Is this GDPR compliant?

Yes. Processing happens exclusively in the EU, privacy by design, data processing agreement included. No data transfer to third countries.

What does a pilot cost?

A pilot starts at around €9,500. The exact price depends on the number of participants and the scope of the project. From the pilot, ongoing diagnostics typically continue as a subscription.

How long does a pilot take?

About a week from setup to report: a conversation window over a few days, then analysis and report in 3 to 5 days. No consulting mandate running for months.

What happens to the data after the project?

Raw data (token and transcript) is deleted automatically 90 days after the campaign ends, and the token-to-email mapping within 24 hours. After the contract ends, all data is deleted or returned within 30 days. Only anonymised, aggregated results remain.

Can consultancies use insivo under their own name?

Yes. Consultancies use insivo as a diagnostic tool inside transformation mandates. The diagnostics do not replace consulting — they provide the data foundation that lets consulting work faster and more precisely.

At which project stage does insivo make sense?

At any stage — from pre-kick-off through delivery to 3 to 6 months before go-live and beyond. It has the most impact before go-live, while readiness gaps can still be closed.

How many participants do we need?

Meaningful from a size that guarantees k-anonymity ≥ 5 per analysis cut. For larger rollouts the campaign scales to hundreds of participants without cost or duration growing linearly.
