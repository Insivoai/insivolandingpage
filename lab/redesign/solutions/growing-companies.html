Built for organizations between roughly 30 and 150 people.

Why this size is different

You don’t have a reporting problem. You have a hearing problem.

Large organizations lose the signal in layers of reporting. At your size there are no layers — but there is also no mechanism. Nobody has time to sit down with sixty people, and the ones who speak up in meetings are not a representative sample of what is actually happening.

Everyone, not a sample

Sixty conversations instead of six

At this size insivo does not sample — it talks to the whole organization. What you get is not “most respondents felt”, it is what your company actually thinks.

No project apparatus

No PMO required

You do not need a transformation office, a steering committee or a change workstream to run this. One person sets it up, everyone else gets a link.

Days, not quarters

A week from question to answer

Conversation window over a few days, analysis and findings in three to five. Short enough that the answer is still current when you act on it.

When companies use it

Four moments where guessing gets expensive.

Introducing or replacing a system

Business Central, Odoo, Weclapp, a new CRM. The rollout is technically done — but half the team still keeps its own spreadsheet, and nobody tells you which half.

Getting AI into daily work

The licences are bought and the kickoff happened. Who is genuinely faster now, who quietly stopped after two weeks, and what would actually help the second group.

Growing faster than your processes

You went from 30 to 90. Responsibilities blur, work gets done twice, and critical knowledge sits with three people who are always busy.

New leadership or a reorganisation

A handover, a new managing director, a restructured team. Everyone says the right things in the town hall. insivo tells you what they say when it is not a town hall.

Confidentiality

In a small company, “anonymous” has to mean something real.

If a report says “the finance team is frustrated” and finance is four people, nobody is anonymous — no matter what the privacy policy says. We treat that as a design constraint, not a footnote.

How it works

No names reach the analysis

Participants join through a token rather than an account. Personal details are stripped automatically before anything is analysed.

What we will not do

No breakdown that identifies a team

Below a minimum group size, findings are reported for the organization as a whole rather than split by team. You lose a slice of granularity and keep the honesty that makes the answers worth having.

Still applies

GDPR does not scale down

EU processing and EU hosting, a data processing agreement, defined deletion periods. Company size changes none of it. If a works council exists, the documentation for it ships as standard.
